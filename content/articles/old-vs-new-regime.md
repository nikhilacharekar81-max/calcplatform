# Old vs. New Tax Regime: Which is Better for FY 2026-27?

Choosing the right income tax regime is a crucial financial decision.

[CALC_TAX_REGIME]

## Key Differences
- **Old Regime:** Allows for deductions (80C, 80D, HRA).
- **New Regime:** Offers lower tax rates but restricts most deductions.

## How to Choose?
If you have high tax-saving investments and expenses (HRA), the old regime might still be beneficial. If you prefer simplicity and have lower deductions, the new regime often results in lower tax liability.
